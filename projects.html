Generative audio robustness evaluation

RE-AMP: Generative Audio Robustness Evaluation

Research motivation: generative audio quality should be evaluated under controlled perturbations and distribution shifts, not only aggregate quality summaries.

Research motivation
Perturbation analysis, robustness curves, distribution shift, compression artifacts, prompt variation, acoustic transformations, ranking instability, and failure-slice analysis.
Method / system contribution
Built a full-stack benchmarking platform with async orchestration, artifact lineage, deterministic manifests, versioned benchmark suites, reusable dataset/model adapters, and comparative dashboards.
Result or evidence
Supported 6,000+ controlled robustness runs, reduced experiment setup time by 89%, and surfaced model-specific failure modes hidden by aggregate MOS-style summaries.
Technologies
Python, PyTorch, FastAPI, React, TypeScript, Redis, PostgreSQL, Docker, async workers, artifact lineage.

Graph learning and spatial-temporal risk

Graph-Based Traffic Collision Risk Modeling

Research motivation: road safety prediction is naturally graph-structured, with risk shaped by topology, neighborhood effects, spatial context, and temporal patterns.

Research motivation
Heterogeneous spatial graph prediction, graph neural networks, spatial-temporal feature construction, network topology, risk ranking, calibration, and comparison against tabular/geospatial/time-windowed baselines.
Method / system contribution
Built a graph ML pipeline over 1M+ collision records and road-network topology, representing intersections and road segments with spatial, temporal, traffic, and structural covariates.
Result or evidence
Improved hotspot-ranking AUROC by more than 20 points using network connectivity, neighborhood structure, centrality features, temporal aggregation, and message passing.
Technologies
Python, PyTorch Geometric, NetworkX, GeoPandas, SQL, XGBoost, scikit-learn, spatial statistics, graph learning.

Robust state-space estimation

Robust Kalman Filtering for Vehicle Localization

Research motivation: localization systems are only useful when recursive estimates remain stable under noisy sensors, dropout, outliers, and covariance misspecification.

Research motivation
Recursive Bayesian estimation, state-space simulation, observability, stability, numerical conditioning, covariance inflation, residual gating, adaptive noise estimation, Huber-style updates, and outlier rejection.
Method / system contribution
Implemented a modular simulation environment with configurable dynamics, observation models, process noise, measurement noise, dropout patterns, delayed sensors, and asynchronous updates.
Result or evidence
Reduced median localization error by 46% and 95th-percentile error by 41% relative to a standard Kalman filter under mixed dropout/outlier regimes.
Technologies
Python, NumPy, SciPy, MATLAB, C++, stochastic simulation, state-space models, numerical methods.
Open GitHub repository

Quantitative research systems

Quant Research Lab

Research motivation: quantitative claims are fragile when backtests ignore leakage, universe changes, future price references, execution assumptions, and point-in-time data constraints.

Research motivation
Factor modeling, walk-forward validation, portfolio optimization, exposure control, rank information coefficients, turnover analysis, attribution, and leakage-aware empirical finance.
Method / system contribution
Built a public-facing factor research and backtesting demo covering point-in-time ingestion, signal construction, portfolio formation, evaluation, attribution, and reproducible tearsheets.
Result or evidence
Added diagnostics for lookahead variables, survivorship effects, future price references, unstable universe definitions, duplicate securities, time-window alignment errors, and transaction-cost sensitivity.
Technologies
Python, FastAPI, pandas, NumPy, SQL, Docker, GitHub Actions, Streamlit, statistical validation, backtesting.
