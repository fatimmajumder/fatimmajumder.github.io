Selected Research Themes

The questions organizing the next stage of my work.

Mathematical Machine Learning

I am interested in the assumptions that make learning algorithms stable, interpretable, and useful beyond benchmark scores. This includes representation learning, graph structure, retrieval, and the gap between empirical behavior and theoretical explanation.

Optimization and Stochastic Approximation

Modern ML depends on iterative procedures whose behavior is often understood empirically before it is understood mathematically. I want to study how stochastic approximation, momentum, adaptive methods, and noisy gradients shape convergence and generalization.

Numerical Linear Algebra and Scientific Computing

Numerical structure determines what can be computed reliably at scale. I am especially drawn to spectral methods, conditioning, approximation, simulation, and the implementation details that decide whether a mathematical model remains faithful in code.

Reliable ML Evaluation and Reproducible Experimentation

Evaluation is a scientific measurement problem, not a reporting layer. My systems work has made datasets, prompts, tokenizers, models, and scoring logic explicit so empirical claims can be replayed, audited, and challenged.
