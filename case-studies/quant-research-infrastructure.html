System Story

What changed when research became shared infrastructure instead of notebook folklore.

Constraint

Strategy work was fragmented across analysts, notebooks, and hand-built review habits.

  • Research workflows needed consistent ingestion, diagnostics, and comparison paths.
  • Validation had to respect point-in-time information constraints, not retrospective convenience.
  • Review needed to include tradability and execution effects, not just attractive backtests.

System design

The platform made research discipline easier to practice.

  • Built the workflow from point-in-time ingestion through factor modeling, walk-forward backtesting, portfolio construction, attribution, and execution simulation.
  • Standardized tearsheets, diagnostics, universe filtering, and experiment tracking.
  • Made lineage, exposure control, and validation gates part of the default review surface.

Outcomes

The platform improved both velocity and legibility across the organization.

  • Strategy research throughput increased by 6.8x.
  • New-analyst ramp time dropped from about 6 weeks to 8 days.
  • Research review became more inspectable because diagnostics and validation were generated consistently.

Why it matters

This is research engineering for teams, not just for isolated experiments.

  • Quant Research Lab is the public-facing version of the same ideas: point-in-time validation, lineage, and execution-aware review.
  • It shows how I build infrastructure that helps more people do serious work correctly.
  • The case study preserves the platform depth without exposing proprietary strategy logic or data.
