System Surface

A research platform instead of a collection of disconnected strategy notebooks.

  • Built the workflow from point-in-time data ingestion through factor modeling, backtesting, portfolio construction, attribution, and execution simulation
  • Standardized tearsheets, diagnostics, and experiment tracking so strategies could be reviewed consistently
  • Made universe filters, sector exposures, and risk controls explicit rather than leaving them buried inside analyst-specific code

Research Discipline

Keep the platform honest about what could have been known at the time.

  • Treated point-in-time data contracts and walk-forward splits as core platform behavior
  • Added leakage-aware validation so factor performance and portfolio quality were evaluated under realistic information constraints
  • Connected exposure control, attribution, and execution simulation so strategy review included tradability, not just backtest returns
  • Made experiment lineage part of review so analysts could replay and compare strategy revisions cleanly

Operational Outcomes

What changed once the platform became shared infrastructure instead of tribal knowledge.

  • Strategy research throughput increased by 6.8x across the organization
  • New-analyst ramp time dropped from roughly 6 weeks to 8 days
  • Research review became more legible because tearsheets, diagnostics, and validation gates were generated consistently
  • The platform supported a 30+ member organization without relying on a few people to manually interpret every pipeline
