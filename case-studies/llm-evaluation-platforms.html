System Surface

Evaluation infrastructure for experiments that changed faster than manual tracking could keep up.

  • Handled model, prompt, dataset, tokenizer, judge, and scoring revisions as first-class lineage inputs instead of ad hoc metadata
  • Supported reasoning, retrieval, multilingual, safety, tool-use, and long-context evaluation lanes inside one platform surface
  • Made benchmark results legible through scenario summaries, slice-level comparisons, artifacts, and replayable manifests

Engineering Decisions

Determinism and auditability were product requirements, not cleanup work.

  • Built asynchronous scheduling, retry semantics, and cache-aware execution so large evaluation batches stayed tractable
  • Structured artifacts and manifests so the exact inputs behind a run were preserved for reruns, comparisons, and review
  • Added run-diff tooling and slice-level regression detection to surface breakages hidden by aggregate scores
  • Used lineage as the contract that made experiments explainable to both engineers and decision-makers

Reliability Outcomes

What changed once the platform could tell the truth about its own experiments.

  • Exact rerun reproducibility increased from 74.1% to 99.96%
  • Median turnaround dropped by 84% and wasted GPU-hours fell by 51%
  • Regression tooling surfaced a preprocessing defect that had inflated internal metrics by 6.1 points
  • Experiment review became auditable enough to support faster iteration without losing confidence in results
