System Surface

Multimodal pipelines with enough structure to trust the result.

  • Built reproducible workflows for clinical variables, imaging features, and multimodal fusion experiments
  • Tracked preprocessing variants, feature sets, training settings, calibration, thresholds, and evaluation outputs
  • Used Python, PyTorch, scikit-learn, SQL, and Streamlit to make the pipeline both rigorous and usable

Methodology Fixes

Most of the gain came from better science, not just a different model.

  • Reworked cohort construction to eliminate patient overlap across splits
  • Removed temporal contamination that was leaking future information into evaluation
  • Standardized held-out evaluation so calibration, subgroup performance, and threshold behavior were visible
  • Treated leakage prevention as infrastructure, not a one-time cleanup

Research Operations

What it took to support 1,000+ controlled experiments.

  • Built repeatable experiment organization so runs could be compared by preprocessing, fusion strategy, or evaluation slice
  • Added interfaces that made it easier for collaborators to inspect metrics and artifacts without re-running everything manually
  • Made subgroup evaluation and threshold analysis part of the default workflow instead of a last-mile extra
