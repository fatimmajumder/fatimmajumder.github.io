System Story

What made the research pipeline scientifically trustworthy.

Constraint

Most of the risk came from methodology, not from a lack of model complexity.

  • Multimodal experiments combined clinical variables, imaging features, and fusion logic across many settings.
  • Patient overlap, temporal contamination, and feature leakage could quietly inflate performance.
  • Collaborators needed repeatable ways to compare runs without rebuilding the pipeline each time.

System design

Leakage prevention and reproducibility became part of the workflow itself.

  • Tracked preprocessing variants, feature sets, fusion strategies, calibration, thresholds, and evaluation outputs.
  • Standardized subgroup evaluation and threshold analysis instead of treating them as last-mile extras.
  • Built interfaces that made metrics and artifacts easier to inspect without re-running everything manually.

Outcomes

Better science produced the largest gains.

  • Held-out AUROC improved from 0.71 to 0.88 after fixing cohort construction and evaluation methodology.
  • More than 1,000 controlled runs were supported across preprocessing, fusion, calibration, and threshold settings.
  • Leakage prevention became infrastructure rather than a one-time cleanup step.

Why it matters

This is the same rigor I bring to any high-stakes ML system.

  • It reinforces the core pattern across my work: strong experimental controls, clean lineage, and high-signal diagnostics.
  • It shows I can work in settings where domain correctness matters as much as model performance.
  • The case study makes the biomedical ML depth on my resume legible without publishing restricted data.
